Dirty Pop
Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026 · Operator: Jaban Studio

한국어 · English

Jaban Studio values the privacy of people who use the mobile game Dirty Pop. This policy explains what information Dirty Pop stores and what information third-party services may process when providing advertising services.

1. Accounts and game data

Dirty Pop does not use registration, login, or its own account server. Stage progress, best records, item quantities, consecutive wins, and sound and vibration settings are stored in WebView storage on your device and are not sent to the operator's server.

Android's automatic backup and device transfer features may include this storage in backups. Actual backup and restoration depend on your Google backup settings, device manufacturer, and transfer environment, so restoration is not guaranteed. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app may erase your game records.

2. Advertising services

Dirty Pop uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to provide banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google may collect or process device identifiers such as the advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and app information, ad impressions and interactions, and performance and diagnostic information for ad delivery, frequency control, performance measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

In regions where legally required, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks for your advertising choices. When UMP requires a privacy options entry point, you can review or change your choices through Privacy options in the game's settings.

3. Rewarded ads

If you choose to watch a rewarded ad, the app checks whether the ad was completed and grants items or additional moves. Dirty Pop does not store this result on its own server.

4. Analytics, payments, and device features

Dirty Pop currently has no separate usage analytics SDK, user accounts, in-app products, or payment features. Vibration and sound effects are device features used to provide game feedback; they do not collect personal information or send it outside the device.

5. Third-party services

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google AdMob privacy guidance

6. Retention and deletion

You can remove game data stored on your device by clearing Dirty Pop's app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app. Retention and deletion of automatic backup copies depend on your Google account and device backup settings. Information Google processes for advertising services is subject to Google's policies and your advertising settings.

7. Children

Dirty Pop is not designed for children under 13, and the operator does not knowingly collect children's personal information directly.

8. International processing and security

Third-party service providers such as Google may process information at facilities around the world. The operator manages the app and third-party services with reasonable care but cannot guarantee the absolute security of electronic storage or transmission.

9. Policy changes

If game features, SDKs, laws, or platform policies change, this policy will be revised and its effective date updated.
